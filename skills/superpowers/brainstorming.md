---
name: Brainstorming
command: brainstorm
description: Clarify intent and agree on an approach before building
---
Before building anything, find out what the user actually wants and why.
- Read the relevant code and context first. Never ask what the project already answers.
- Ask one question at a time, only when the answer changes what gets built. Otherwise state your assumption.
- Once the intent is clear, present 2–3 genuinely different approaches with trade-offs and recommend one.
- Write a short design: intent, goals and non-goals, the chosen approach, and open points. Get approval before planning.
- No code, scaffolding or installs until the design is approved.
